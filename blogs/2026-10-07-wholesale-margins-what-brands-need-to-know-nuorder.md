Wholesale margins can look healthy on paper and still subtly shrink in practice, chipped away by supplier terms, pricing missteps, and operational inefficiencies that add up over time. For brands selling at scale, even small margin erosion compounds into a real hit to profitability. This article breaks down what a healthy wholesale margin actually looks like, the factors that most commonly erode it, and the practical steps brands can take to protect it going forward.
