This August marked our fifth edition alongside Mandatory during Copenhagen Fashion Week, bringing together brands, buyers, and the wider fashion community once again. Over the course of our partnership, Copenhagen has become more than just a moment to connect with the industry. It’s an opportunity to exchange perspectives on how wholesale is changing, and to hear directly from brands and buyers about what they need next.
