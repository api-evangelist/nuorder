# NuORDER by Lightspeed

> NuORDER is a global B2B commerce platform (owned by Lightspeed) for wholesale buying and selling between brands and retailers. It combines a fully-branded wholesale eCommerce storefront, an Assortments planning tool for data-driven buying, and a Marketplace connecting buyers to 3,000+ brands across fashion, footwear, and apparel. A partner-gated REST API at api.nuorder.com syncs products, orders, and customer data with ERP, PIM, and other commerce systems.
